# Rooftop movement at 30 fps

## Investigation · 2026-11-28

The player asked which of the sixteen failed checks affect the feel of movement. The comparison uses the rooftop gym, Godot 4.7.2 and the project's 60 Hz physics with rendering fixed at 30 or 60 fps. Keep the approved expressions and movement tuning as the baseline. The planning pass uses command-line diagnostics. Implementation requires player review of this plan.

The shared test helper waits once for physics and once for rendering on each iteration. A standalone clock probe measured 62 physics ticks for 31 of those waits at 30 fps, versus 31 ticks at 60 fps. Waiting for physics alone measured 31 ticks at both rates. An input made after those paired waits therefore moves by over half a second between runs. Pose checks also need to sample after the animation mixer or the skeleton has applied the pose for that rendered frame.

### What the sixteen checks cover

| Checks | What a player might notice | How to judge the result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2. Wall catch after releasing the stick, and reversing away before the catch | An unwanted grab or a missed catch | Apply the same approach, release and reversal on the same ticks. Verify both the starting distance and whether a grip is secured. |
| 3–4. Buffered landing jump and running jump without continued steering | A second jump feels swallowed, or Chibi sticks to a wall during a plain jump | Match the release and landing window. Record takeoff, landing, animation choice and wall attachment. |
| 5. Full-speed vault | Her hand misses the rail or the torso moves ahead of the vault pose | Compare the applied clip with the interpolated body, then check the actual planted hand against the rail. |
| 6. Slide under the gate | She stands up early or catches on the ceiling | Record slide entry, the dive playing once, capsule height under the gate, and where she can stand. |
| 7–8. Wall-run gap correction and wall kick | She snaps sideways or the kick loses lift | Measure displacement per physics tick, clearance, forward speed and launch velocity. Check the kick pose separately on a displayed frame. |
| 9–12. Neutral wind drift, Flow credit, scarf fade on exit, and loaded-glide respawn | Inconsistent carry, unearned Flow, a scarf that snaps shut, or wind that persists after respawn | Reuse the same wind phase, entry speed, steering sequence and exit point. Sample wind state and carry before and after respawn. |
| 13. Upside-down rope grab and reverse | The head pops through the rope for one frame | Label every displayed sample as attached or released. Measure head and grips after skeleton modifiers, including the first grab frame. |
| 14–16. Strap transition, snorkel seating, and rocky-bank swim entry | Gear flashes, the mouthpiece floats, or Chibi misses the water transition | Replay the bank approach by physics tick. Check wading, swim entry, strap visibility and mouthpiece seating over the actual transition. These three checks run in the shared rocky-basin example. |

The original suite reproduced 324/340 at 30 fps and 340/340 at 60. Temporary copies scheduled the inputs in physics ticks and sampled the vault after the mixer had consumed its seek. The full run then passed 340/340 at **each** rate with the same gameplay code and tolerances. At full Flow the vault hand was 1.4 cm from the rail and the maximum displayed phase error was zero to three decimals at both rates. The wall-run gap was 29.5 cm and the largest one-tick correction was 4.8 cm in each run.

In a separate wind replay the four failures cleared with the same 1,031 physics callbacks at each rate. The neutral glide carried Chibi at 1.00 m/s in the lull and 3.00 m/s at the peak. She coasted out at 1.98 m/s, lost the canopy load and earned zero uncommanded Flow. The loaded-glide respawn cleared both the wind state and the scarf. Changes to wind strength or damping would alter the working 60 fps feel as well.

On equivalent inputs, the wall catch secured on tick 23 after release. Reversing while still out of reach left Chibi free at both rates. The landing jump relaunched five physics ticks after the press. The slide stayed low at tick 35 and recovered 22 ticks later at the same spot. The wall kick had the same sampled velocity at both rates. The original 30 fps test sampled it nine physics ticks later, after gravity had reduced its upward speed.

The upside-down rope is the strongest visual candidate. Measuring the head bone after the skeleton applied its pose exposed a brief clearance shortfall at **both** rates, even when the full temporary suite was green. The closest head-center distances below the rope were 17.1 and 17.3 cm for the two 30 fps start offsets and 16.9 cm at 60 fps. The current test asks for 18 cm. The first displayed grab cleared by 22.6–27.1 cm at 30 fps, and the reversal stayed near 26.9 cm. No attached head-center sample crossed the cable. A separate mesh probe found a head-weighted face vertex 31 mm from the centerline while attached at 30 fps. The cable radius is 32 mm, giving about 1 mm of geometric overlap. Its visibility from the gameplay camera is unverified. Review normal-speed footage from the play camera and a side close-up. If the silhouette touches, add the smallest head or upper-body pose correction during that part of the hang and recheck both grips, foot contact and the 180-degree reversal.

The bank replay reached swim on physics tick 35 at 30 fps. After 180 ticks Chibi was still in the water, the strap had stayed opaque through its color change and the mouthpiece was fully seated. The original 30 fps run continued beyond the intended section of the basin and finished on land, making its gear assertions describe a different point along the route.

### Proposed implementation and review

- [x] Schedule jump, steer, slide, wind exposure and bank approach by physics ticks. Check input edges on the intended tick, and check the starting distance or wind phase before drawing conclusions. Update the affected waits in `tests/run_tests.gd`, `tests/dynamic_wall_checks.gd`, `tests/wind_tunnel_regressions.gd`, `tests/wind_assisted_glide_regressions.gd` and `tests/water_regressions.gd`. Keep waits for UI drawing and scene teardown where they belong.
- [x] Record what was displayed. For the vault, use the mantle test’s applied animation sampling and independently check the hand on the rail. For the rope, sample after skeleton modifiers, from the first attached pose through reversal. For the gear, measure the strap and mouthpiece across equip and stow. Require enough distinct render samples to cover each action at 30 fps, including the first and last poses.
- [x] Replay the same inputs at both render rates with 60 Hz physics, then shift input arrival by one physics tick to exercise both halves of a 30 fps frame. Keep the existing distance, clearance, pose and Flow limits. Run the full suite at 30 and 60 fps and once with variable frame timing, plus the manual and automatic expression checks. Record matched clips of a wall jump/kick, full-speed vault, upside-down grab/reversal and bank entry for player review.
- [x] Change a player-facing setting or animation only after a matched replay shows a missed action, a visible bad pose or a repeatable discontinuity. Isolate the affected move, compare a before/after clip and keep the approved 60 fps behavior as a control. If both rates meet the checks and the motion looks right, close this target with the test changes.

## Implementation and verification · 2026-11-28

The player approved the implementation plan. The comparison reel shows the wall jump and kick, full-Flow vault, scarf glide and rocky-bank transition at their native render rates, using the same 60 Hz physics inputs. The bank reached PoolSwim on replay tick 47 in every run. With a one-tick offset the starting floor sample moved less than a millimetre at swim entry. The other three routes matched in state, transition tick and recorded position across both rates and both offsets. [Matched movement reel](<C:/Users/count/.codex/visualizations/2026/11/26/01a2195a-164a-74f3-b7e4-5e26d3c07124/rooftop-movement-review/Rooftop movement at 30 and 60 fps.mp4>).

Input waits now advance one physics tick per iteration. The vault measures the animation after its seek has applied and requires distinct rendered samples from entry to release. The swim test samples the opaque strap, mouth pose and gear visibility after skeleton updates across equip and stow. A concurrent water-receiver edit needed an explicit Object type to compile. The verification used the shared project with that annotation.

The upside-down crawl now lowers the head by 17 mm in the displayed skeleton pose. The character root, attachment reach, hand and foot positions and zipline pose stay at their original values. The skinned head/face probe against the 32 mm cable radius changed from about 1 mm of overlap to 14.6 mm clearance at 30 fps and 17.7 mm at 60 fps. Both one-tick entry offsets passed. The four grip measurements stayed within the original 6 cm tolerance. The close side view shows the neck remains joined to the scarf and torso.

Releasing a loaded crawl rope exposed a second visible issue. The station moved the lowest point of the bend to the endpoint as soon as Chibi let go. Now the existing spring rebounds from the release position while the bend eases toward the middle. The measured center segment previously rose 317 mm in two physics ticks after a centered release. It now rises 18.5 mm in the same interval at both render rates. An off-center release check verifies the initial rebound and the eventual centered resting curve. The zipline still resets its pulley to the start.

Final verification passed 370/370 at [30 fps](<C:/Users/count/.codex/visualizations/2026/11/26/01a2195a-164a-74f3-b7e4-5e26d3c07124/rooftop-movement-review/approved-movement-30.log>), [60 fps](<C:/Users/count/.codex/visualizations/2026/11/26/01a2195a-164a-74f3-b7e4-5e26d3c07124/rooftop-movement-review/approved-movement-60.log>) and [variable frame timing](<C:/Users/count/.codex/visualizations/2026/11/26/01a2195a-164a-74f3-b7e4-5e26d3c07124/rooftop-movement-review/approved-movement-0.log>). All three expression suites passed at both fixed rates. Desktop and touch title-to-rooftop checks passed. The fresh Windows release passed menu navigation, expression interruption and rope-release replay from its exported pack. The standalone executable starts headlessly. The player approved the finished rooftop movement and rope changes on 2026-11-28 and requested publication to main.
